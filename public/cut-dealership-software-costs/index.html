How to Cut Dealership Software Costs

Dealers are paying for software they could build themselves. Every year another tool shows up with a monthly fee: a texting add-on, a follow-up tool, a review responder, a desk helper. Each one makes sense on its own. Together they add up, and most of them do a narrow job that a well-set-up AI agent can now handle.

Start with a simple audit. List every add-on subscription your store pays for, and next to each one write what it actually does day to day. You'll find that many of them write messages, summarize information or remind someone to do something. Those are exactly the jobs AI agents handle well.

Keep your DMS and CRM. They're the system of record, and this isn't about replacing them. The goal is to stop paying monthly for the small extras around them when your own staff can run the same thing with AI they already have access to.
