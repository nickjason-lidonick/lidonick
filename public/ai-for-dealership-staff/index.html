Who We Help: AI for Every Seat in the Dealership

Lidonick helps every seat in the store. You don't need a dealer-group IT department or a big software budget. If you're a salesperson who wants help staying on top of follow-ups, a GM who wants a better morning, or a service director who's tired of building reports, we'll help you set up your own AI agents for your role. It's a one-time fee, with no subscription.

We work with franchise stores and independent or used car dealerships alike. What matters is the work, not the size of the store.

AI for car salespeople

Stay on top of follow-ups, draft texts and emails faster, prep before a customer walks in, and keep your pipeline organized.

  • A daily follow-up list built from your prospect export, showing who you haven't talked to, who drove a car and hasn't decided, and who's a be-back
  • Draft follow-up texts and emails in your voice for you to edit and send
  • Pre-appointment prep: a quick summary of a customer's history and the vehicle they're interested in before they arrive

Good AI prompts for car sales are specific, personal, and never quote a price that isn't on the listing. See our Grok Bot prompts for car dealerships.

AI for sales managers

See how each person is pacing against goal and get the numbers you need without building the report yourself.

  • Pace by salesperson against the month's goal, pulled from the reports you already run
  • Open-deal and follow-up gaps so you know who needs coaching today
  • Desk prep summaries before the sales meeting

AI for dealership general managers

Start each morning with a brief on units, pace against manufacturer objectives and selling days, salesperson performance, the service drive, and anything that needs your attention.

  • The GM morning brief built from the reports, CSVs, and forwarded emails you already get
  • On-demand questions like "How many used units are over 60 days?" answered from your own data
  • Escalation alerts when a customer complaint lands in a shared inbox

AI for service directors and service departments

Keep an eye on reviews and customer complaints, catch problems on the drive sooner, and cut down on daily reporting and email.

  • Review alerts for Google, Yelp, and other sites, with a draft reply for your approval
  • Complaint monitoring across service inboxes, sorted by urgency
  • Next-day drive prep covering waiters, loaners, and jobs waiting on parts

AI for F&I managers

Speed up the prep, paperwork, and follow-up that keep you from your next customer.

  • Funding and paperwork checklists showing which deals are waiting on stips or signatures
  • Product presentation prep in plain language, where the manager decides what's said to the customer
  • Careful data handling. F&I deals with sensitive customer financial information, so we keep agents to the minimum data they need, in line with your written security program under the FTC Safeguards Rule (16 CFR Part 314).

AI for the BDC and internet sales

Speed up the prep, paperwork, and follow-up that keep you from your next customer.

  • First-response drafts for new internet leads, approved by a rep before they're sent
  • Appointment confirmation drafts for tomorrow's showroom and service appointments
  • A missed-lead sweep at the end of each day

Shopping for an "AI BDC"? Many of those are subscription products. Before you sign another contract, it's worth seeing what your team can set up in AI tools they already have.

FAQ

Which dealership roles can use AI agents?

Any role that spends time on reports, follow-ups, inboxes, or reviews. That includes salespeople, sales managers, GMs, service directors, F&I managers, and BDC staff.

Can a single salesperson work with Lidonick, or only the whole store?

Either. We help individuals in any role, as well as managers who want help for their whole team.

Do you work with independent and used car dealerships?

Yes. The work is the same whether you're a franchise store or an independent, and the setup uses AI tools you already have.

How can AI help a dealership service department?

Common examples are review alerts, complaint monitoring, and next-day drive prep, built from the exports and inboxes your service team already uses.

Is AI safe for F&I?

It can be, with care. We keep F&I agents to the minimum data they need and recommend checking any setup against your written security program under the FTC Safeguards Rule.
