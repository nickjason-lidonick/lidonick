AI for F&I Managers

F&I is detail work under time pressure. Deal notes, lender requirements, product explanations and follow-ups all have to be right, and the customer is waiting.

An AI agent can help with the prep. It can summarize a deal jacket so you walk in ready, draft plain-language explanations of the products you offer in your own words, build a checklist of what each lender needs, and draft follow-ups for missing stips. You stay in control of every decision and every conversation with the customer.

Your F&I team can build this with AI tools they already have access to. Lidonick helps F&I managers set up their own agents and prompts for a one-time fee, with no subscriptions and no long-term contracts. Book a call to talk through your process.
