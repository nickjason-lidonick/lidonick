AI for Service Advisors

Service advisors spend a big part of the day writing: explaining recommended work to customers, sending status updates, following up on declined repairs, and turning tech notes into plain language. All of it matters, and all of it takes time away from the drive.

An AI agent can draft those messages for them. Give it the tech's notes and it writes a clear, friendly explanation the customer can understand. It can draft follow-ups on declined work and summarize a vehicle's history before the customer arrives. The advisor reviews it, adjusts it and sends it.

This doesn't need a new subscription or a new system. Your advisors can set it up with AI tools they already have, as long as the instructions are right.
